# Big Idea A2: review notes

50 original exercises. The CSV preserves the approved two-question, 17-column format: text type first, main idea second. IDs continue from big-idea-105.

Passages use three body paragraphs and accessible A2 language. Email headings and greetings are additional lines. The main-idea options follow the approved example: two true but incomplete details and one reversed or mistaken whole-text account. Options remain in named CSV columns because the game shuffles them.

The passages describe imagined people, events, products and reported results. No real research results, quotations or named organisations are claimed.

The language level is an authoring target; classroom use can establish how demanding the exercises are for particular readers.
